# FLUXATLAS launch-film design

## Style Prompt
A precise laboratory instrument on warm archival paper. Large, calm statements lead into actual neutron-field evidence and the functioning observatory. Fine rules, strict alignment and technical coordinates carry the scientific identity. The film is an invitation to inspect a reproducible experiment, not a claim of professional certification.

## Colors
- Paper `#f4f2e9`, main canvas.
- Ink `#172d36`, titles and frame.
- Ember `#d86435`, reference marker and small emphases.
- Blue `#3a7383`, learned-model marker.
- Surface `#faf9f4`, numerical field panels.

## Typography
System Helvetica Neue/sans for clear statements, Georgia italic for the observatory register, ui-monospace for numerical labels. These trace the existing product source and remain local/deterministic.

## Motion
20 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps. Short entrances into long settled holds. 0.5-second crossfades at actual topic changes. Numerical maps are fixed exported arrays; motion reveals panels without altering data. No animated particle tracks are presented as measured histories.

## What NOT to Do
- No fabricated accuracy, speedup, data, instrument measurements or particle histories.
- No resolution-independent neural-operator claim.
- No neon, glass cards, generic particle effects, gradient text or AI illustration.
- No safety-analysis or dose-rate implication.
